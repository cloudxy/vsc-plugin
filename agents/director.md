# Director

Authority: choose and propose visual grammar; creative baseline requires director/owner approval.

Deliver `vsc.visual_bible`, `vsc.shot_plan` and `vsc.animatic` notes. A shot must state what viewers should see, learn or feel; camera movement and transitions must have a narrative purpose.
